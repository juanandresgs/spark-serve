# Single-Spark Qwen3.8-Flash-Next: next optimization experiments

Research note, 2026-09-28. Scope is one DGX Spark, TensorFold 0.3.6.1, the 3.05 bpw EXL3 pack, 262,144 total tokens, four slots and MTP6. These are **independent experiment designs**, not deployed changes or copied community implementations. External measurements are leads, not speedup forecasts for this stack.

## Established baseline and decision

The same-device cooperative 2,048-row trial cut short-request p95 time to first token (TTFT) from **356.36 to 2.73 seconds** behind two 131K-token prefills; the longest active-stream pause fell from **362.737 to 2.867 seconds** in a separate packaged-image comparison. Whole-workload time changed by +1.22% and +0.67%, respectively. This fixes most measured *interference*, with four occupied slots and bursty output still limiting responsiveness. A fresh **253,841-input/8,192-output-reserve** request still took **536.45 seconds** to first token, versus **535.13 seconds** on the original image. The 4,096-row arm took 533.38 seconds, missed its predeclared 5% improvement target, and recorded a recovered allocation warning. The 8K diagnostic assigned 7.79/12.51 instrumented seconds to MoE, but first-use and synchronization costs make that fraction invalid as a 254K profile. These figures come from the September 28 bounded trial and its research analysis; raw receipts and operational identifiers are intentionally omitted here.

**Decision:** keep the cooperative 2,048-row candidate as the scheduling baseline; investigate cold work independently. A cold-speed claim needs at least **20% repeatable TTFT reduction** (roughly 107 seconds on this fixture), with no more than 5% decode regression and preserved mixed-load behavior. Profiling itself is the first experiment, not a claimed optimization.

## Experimental controls and order

Use one fixed, content-addressed checkpoint and runtime per A/B pair. Fix actual input tokens, 8,192-token output reservation, template, draft setting, seeds, cache semantics and page-cache state. An identical prompt replay is **not** a TensorFold prefix hit; only a strict extension of a retained snapshot qualifies. For kernel arithmetic, compare greedy and sampled token IDs against the serial path, plus near-limit retrieval/continuation and typed streaming tools. Track CUDA/OOM faults and at least 5 GiB available memory throughout. Repeat baseline/candidate in alternating order; report run-level numbers, medians and dispersion, not an unsupported confidence interval. Preserve the cooperative mixed-load gains and no more than 10% bulk-completion loss while assessing cold speed.

The fastest path to a useful decision is: **long-context profile → one causal ablation → one isolated implementation → matched end-to-end crossover**. If MoE dominates, prioritize grouped EXL3 work; if major mapped-page faults dominate, prioritize cache policy and PLE; if attention/indexer dominates, work there. The alternative runtime screen can proceed as a separate model-pack qualification, with no assumption that published 137–161s figures transfer to this checkpoint or fixture. The combined 4,096-row/head-skip arm missed its cold-speed threshold, and the selected 2,048-row/head-skip bundle still measured 536.45s. The head-skip contribution was not isolated. Deeper MTP primarily targets decode, so these should not lead the next cold-speed trial.
